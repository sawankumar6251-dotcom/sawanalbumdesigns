About Sawan Album Design

Sawan Album Design focuses on one thing: wedding albums that photographers are proud to hand over. We design custom albums for individual clients, and build ready-to-use PSD resources — album templates, text sets and cliparts — for photographers and designers who design their own.

Every file we release is checked for how it actually holds up inside a real project: organized layers, sensible print sizes, and layouts that leave room for your own photos to lead.
